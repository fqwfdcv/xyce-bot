Privacy Policy for Xyce Music Bot Effective Date: 27 September 2026 Xyce Music Bot ("Bot") is a Discord music bot that allows users to play audio content in Discord voice channels. Information We Collect The Bot may process: - Discord User IDs - Discord Server (Guild) IDs - Discord Channel IDs - Music search queries and commands sent to the Bot How We Use Information We use this information solely to: - Provide music playback functionality - Process commands - Maintain bot performance and stability - Prevent abuse and troubleshoot errors Data Storage The Bot does not permanently store personal user data. Temporary data such as queues and playback information may be stored in memory while the Bot is running and is automatically removed when no longer needed. Data Sharing The Bot does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties except when required by law. Third-Party Services The Bot may use third-party services such as: - Discord - YouTube - Spotify These services have their own privacy policies. Data Removal Users may stop using the Bot at any time.
