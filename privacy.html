Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 27, 2026

Xyce Music Bot ("Bot") is a Discord music bot that provides music playback functionality inside Discord servers.

Information We Collect

  • Discord User IDs
  • Discord Server IDs
  • Discord Channel IDs
  • Commands sent to the Bot
  • Music search queries

How We Use Information

The collected information is used solely to:

  • Provide music playback features
  • Process user commands
  • Maintain service stability
  • Troubleshoot issues

Data Storage

The Bot does not permanently store personal user information. Temporary playback data may be stored in memory while the Bot is running.

Third-Party Services

The Bot may interact with Discord, YouTube, Spotify, and other third-party services.

Data Sharing

No personal information is sold, rented, or shared with third parties except where required by law.
